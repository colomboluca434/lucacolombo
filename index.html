Luca Colombo

Professor of Economics
Affiliated with the Centre for Economics at Paris-Saclay (CEPS)
I am an economist working in industrial organization, environmental and resource economics, and dynamic games. My research examines strategic decision-making in dynamic environments, with applications to oligopolistic competition, pricing, natural-resource management, environmental regulation, and social networks.
