Luca Colombo

Professor of Economics
Affiliated with the Centre for Economics at Paris-Saclay (CEPS)

My research focuses on industrial organization, environmental and resource economics, and dynamic games. I study strategic interactions in dynamic environments, with particular emphasis on oligopolistic competition, pricing, natural-resource management, environmental regulation, and international environmental agreements.
